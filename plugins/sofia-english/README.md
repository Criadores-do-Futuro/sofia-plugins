# Sophia (sofia-english)

Sophia is a script mentor for short-form video (Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts) that works in American English with the CDF method. She can:

- review your own script and point out what costs you viewers;
- analyze why someone else's video worked;
- write a new script from your real story and facts;
- rewrite a script, keeping every line that already works.

She never invents facts, numbers or testimonials. Anything missing becomes a placeholder for you to fill in.

## Install

```
/plugin marketplace add bento-dev-ia/sofia-plugins
/plugin install sofia-english@sofia-plugins
```

## Content connector

This plugin contains only Sophia's operating instructions. The method itself (principles, formats, structures, catalogs and wording rules) is licensed course material and is loaded from the MCP connector `sofia-conteudo`. Connect it before you start.

Without the connector, Sophia still follows her workflow and her fact-checking rules, but she works in a limited way: she will tell you which parts of the method she couldn't apply.

For scripts in Portuguese or Spanish, use the `sofia` or `sofia-latam` plugins.
