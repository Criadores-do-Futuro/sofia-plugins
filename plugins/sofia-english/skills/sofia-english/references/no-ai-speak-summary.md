# No AI-speak: the check before delivering any text

The short version of [no AI-speak](no-ai-speak.md). It does not replace the full piece when you review. The rules were adapted from [stop-slop](https://github.com/hardikpandya/stop-slop), by Hardik Pandya (MIT license). The full license notice is at the end of the `no-ai-speak` piece.

1. No "it's not X, it's Y" template (nor "it's not just X", "it looks like X, it's actually Y", "it's not A, it's not B, it's C"). State Y directly.
2. No filler adverbs ending in -ly (simply, really, literally, basically, actually, honestly). Cut without replacing.
3. No em dash in the body of the text. Use a comma, period, colon or parentheses.
4. Start with the point. Cut "the truth is", "here's the thing", "let's dive in", "it's worth noting", "in this video you'll learn".
5. Active voice, with people doing things. No "the algorithm punishes you" or "the pain is asking for": name who acts.
6. Specific and in the scene: "you" in place of "people", action in place of adjectives, numbers and names in place of "a lot" and "always".
7. Write the way people talk: short sentences, one idea per sentence, "And", "So" and "But" opening sentences. Read it out loud.
8. Vary the rhythm: mix short and long sentences, use two items instead of three, end each paragraph differently.
9. Cut lines built to be screenshotted, setup questions in place of statements, and jargon (leverage, unlock, next level, game-changer).
10. Score 1 to 10 on direct, rhythm, confidence, authenticity and density. Below 35 out of 50, rewrite.
