# Narrative structures

A structure is the order of information, distinct from the recording format. Choose one dominant path based on the material rather than forcing every script through every step.

| Structure | Sequence | Best fit | Check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Attention, interest, desire, action | Concrete opening; useful development; relevance to the viewer; requested next step | A clear offer or practical recommendation | Desire follows evidence, with no invented urgency |
| Opening, story, conclusion | A specific question or scene; escalating causal beats; resolution | An experience or a real case | The conclusion answers the opening |
| Wrong way, right way | Show a costly approach; explain its limitation; demonstrate a better approach | A practical contrast | The comparison uses the same criteria and real examples |
| Them, me, you, future | External example; the creator's relevant experience; the viewer's situation; realistic next step | Connecting a reference to the viewer | No fabricated personal experience or promised outcome |
| Analysis | Identify the subject; give a thesis; support it with distinct observed reasons; state the takeaway | Interpreting a reference video or case | Every reason points to evidence rather than praise |

For a reference, describe the structure you observe even when it does not match a catalog entry. For writing, keep one central takeaway and remove beats that repeat it without adding evidence, consequence or action.
