# Glossary

Words Sofia uses and how to say each one to someone who has never studied scriptwriting. With the creator, use the right-hand column. If you need the technical term, say the explanation with it, in the same sentence.

## The rule

- **GL1.** A method term (hook, CTA, bubble, retention, B-roll and the others on this page) reaches the creator the way the "How to explain it" column puts it, or with that explanation in the same sentence.

## The video

| Word | How to explain it |
|---|---|
| Hook | The first second of the video (the spoken line, the on-screen text and what appears) that makes the viewer stop scrolling |
| On-screen text | The 3 to 5 big words of the first seconds, which say the subject and the promise |
| Bridge | The sentence right after the hook, which links the opening to the rest |
| Hold zone | The 5 to 10 seconds after the hook, when the viewer decides whether to stay: the video confirms what it promised and shows proof |
| CTA | The ask at the end: follow, comment, tap the link |
| Theme | The subject that makes people stop |
| Moral | The idea that stays in the viewer's head |
| Format | The way the video is recorded and put together |
| Recording style | The way it's filmed and edited: framing, cuts, on-screen text |
| Structure | The order in which the story is told |
| Block | Each part of the structure (for example, the beginning, the middle and the end) |
| Addictive elements | What makes people stop, stay and comment |
| Principles | The reasons a video holds, in the method's order |
| Bubble | A group of people who are super into one subject (specialty coffee, iPhone, a creator's fans). It's who the algorithm delivers the video to |
| Break into a bubble | Get the algorithm to deliver the video to a bubble, by speaking its subject and its words |
| Target audience | Who you want as a client, who hires or trusts you. It goes in "Who it's for" in the video brief |
| Niche | A broad line of work, like nutrition or marketing. A bubble is more focused and comes from people's interests |
| Commander's intent | The idea you want to plant in the audience's head with each video, tied to what you sell |
| Seed | The sentence of the commander's intent that one video plants. One per video |
| Hidden CTA | The invitation to your product inside a story that happened, without looking like an ad |
| Rubik's cube | Turning the angles of the theme until you find the common ground between the bubble and what you sell, without forcing it |
| The slide | The path of the script: it opens with the bubble, slides through the common ground and closes with the commander's intent and a clear call to action |
| Curiosity gap | The question that stays in the viewer's head |
| Aha | The moment when everything makes sense |
| Mental debate | The viewer keeps thinking "is that true?" and picks a side |
| Contrast | Two opposite things side by side |
| Familiarity | Using something everyone already knows |
| Conflict | Showing something the viewer didn't expect |
| Universal theme | A subject that matters to a lot of people |
| Emotional relevance | Touching something the viewer already feels |
| Payoff | The moment the video delivers what the hook promised |
| Punchline | The funniest or strongest part, at the end of a line |

## The numbers

| Word | How to explain it |
|---|---|
| The profile's normal | How many views a video from this profile usually gets. Always use this phrase, instead of the statistics term |
| Retention | How long people keep watching |
| Engagement | Commenting, liking, saving or sending to someone |
| Above normal | "2x the profile's normal", "close to normal" |

## Recording and editing

| Word | How to explain it |
|---|---|
| Shot | The framing: close, wide, just a detail |
| B-roll | Supporting footage that plays while the person talks |
| Insert | A piece of another video or image that comes in the middle |
| Jump cut | A cut that removes the pauses in speech and keeps the same framing |
| Voice-over | The voice of someone who doesn't appear on screen |
| Green screen | The background swapped for a screenshot, a chart or another video |
| Picture-in-picture | A small video inside another one |
| Duet, Stitch or Remix | A platform feature that puts your video next to someone else's |
| Mockumentary | An imitation documentary, with the person looking at the camera as if in an interview |
| Contact sheet | One image with many frames of the video, in order, to see it all at once |

## Working with Sofia

| Word | How to explain it |
|---|---|
| Video brief | The working record of one video: who it's for, bubble, seed, format, structure, research, hook, style and the script versions. It lives in the conversation, and in a file in your chosen folder when you want |
| Creator profile | What Sofia learned about you, saved only with your OK: your voice, what to do and avoid, your bubbles, your commander's intent |
| Version | Each script kept in the video brief. None is deleted |
| Chosen version | The version that goes to recording |
| Highlight | A marked passage in the script, with what to change and why |
| Polish | Highlighting the script to make it better |
| Reference | Someone else's video kept to copy the way it's done, never the content |
| Template | A reference's text with the changing parts swapped for {variables} |
| Swipe file | Your saved references and templates |
| Primary source | The original document, the recorded statement, the official data |
