# Commander's intent (summary)

Commander's intent is the idea the creator wants to plant in the audience's mind with each video, the thread between positioning and offer. It's the second circle of [breaking into a bubble](breaking-the-bubble.md) and the moral of [theme and moral](addictive-elements/theme-and-moral.md). The full piece is [commander's intent](commanders-intent.md).

- **3 pillars:** the leader's Authenticity (the creator is also the product), Movement (a cause bigger than them, with a common enemy) and Product (mechanisms with their own names, a new solution to an old problem that explains why the earlier ones failed). Each one lives in a section of the creator profile: "Commander's intent: Authenticity", "Commander's intent: Movement" and "Commander's intent: Product".
- **One seed per script,** from one pillar, written in the video brief's idea ("Seed (Movement): [the sentence]"). Seeds add up over the weeks.
- **Authenticity and movement enter inside the story,** with no pause for an ad.
- **The close reinforces a belief of the movement.**
- **Hidden CTA in most videos,** inside a story that happened ("Last week, someone in my community asked me..."): the viewer registers the authority and that there's a service to hire. **Direct CTA in about 2 out of every 10 videos** ("I have a method built around exactly this. Tap the link in my bio to grab your spot.").
- No mechanism, story or belief the creator didn't tell you.
