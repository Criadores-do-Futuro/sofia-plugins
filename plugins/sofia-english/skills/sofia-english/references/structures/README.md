# Script structures

Tested structures for writing short videos that spread. Each one fits a different kind of video.

The structure is the order of the story. The format is the way it is recorded. Every script has both, and the format piece (`formats/<id>`, see [formats](../formats/README.md)) says which structure fits best. Each structure has its own piece (`structures/<id>`).

Only the five structures below exist. When a format asks for something of its own (an exchange of lines, a look into the camera, a list of reasons), it uses one of these five, and the format piece says how.

## Available structures

| Structure | Steps | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| [AIDA](aida.md) | Attention, Interest, Desire, Action | Selling, content that teaches and leads to a request |
| [ISC](identification-story-content.md) | Identification, Story, Content | Connection, planting an idea without sounding like a pitch |
| [Wrong Way, Right Way](wrong-way-right-way.md) | Conflict, Wrong way, Turning point, Right way, Change | Teaching a better way to do something, tutorials, authority, contrast |
| [Them, Me, You, Future](them-me-you-future.md) | Them, Me, You, Future | A story that starts outside and ends with the viewer, a narrative that builds |
| [Analysis](analysis.md) | Conflict, Results, 3 reasons, Moral | Riding someone who is going viral, analyzing creators, teaching your own method |

## Writing the structure into the video brief

Before any spoken text, write the chosen structure in the video brief: one block per step, in order, in short bullets tied to the research findings (facts with sources, bubble comments, references). The spoken script comes later. Format, structure, addictive elements and principles must fit together. The creator approves the structure before the hook.

## Checks that apply to every structure

- Choose one dominant path from the material. Do not force every script through every step of every structure.
- Keep one central takeaway. Cut blocks that repeat it without adding evidence, consequence or action.
- When analyzing a reference, describe the structure you actually observe, even when it does not match one of the five. Say which one it comes closest to.
- No block invents urgency, a personal experience or a result. Desire and change follow real evidence.

## How the team uses these structures

- The **scriptwriter** suggests the structure that best fits the creator's theme and goal.
- When analyzing a video, Sofia identifies which structure it uses.
- The **critic** checks whether the chosen structure was applied the way its piece asks.
