# AIDA structure

**Attention → Interest → Desire → Action.**

## Overview

Your video has to grab Attention, build Interest, create Desire for the idea you want to sell and lead the audience to an Action (your CTA).

## Steps

### 1. Attention

An interesting theme, joined to a curiosity or a conflict inside that theme. The theme sets how far the video can go; the curiosity or the conflict becomes the hook (see [hook](../hook.md)).

### 2. Interest

Set up the proof. Tell a story, make an argument, cite a study, teach something. The viewer stays because they are learning or getting involved.

### 3. Desire

The moral of the story. The viewer believes the idea, feels they need the product, gains hope or swaps a belief. This is the moment to feel. Desire follows evidence: no invented urgency.

### 4. Action

The closing request, the CTA. Direct (follow, comment, tap the link) or indirect (implied, which sounds more elegant).

What AIDA asks for: a theme the bubble cares about, an opening with curiosity or conflict, a middle that proves the point, a moral that creates desire and the request at the end.

## When to use it

- Sales videos (a product, a service, an idea)
- Educational content that leads to a conclusion
- Short videos that need a clear structure

## Quick checklist

- **ST.aida.1.** Is the theme interesting to the bubble?
- **ST.aida.2.** Does the opening have curiosity or conflict?
- **ST.aida.3.** Does the middle prove the point with a story, data or teaching?
- **ST.aida.4.** Does the moral create emotional desire?
- **ST.aida.5.** Is the CTA there (direct or indirect)?
