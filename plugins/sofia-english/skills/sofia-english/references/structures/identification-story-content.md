# ISC structure (Identification, Story, Content)

**Identification → Story → Content.**

## Overview

Guides the viewer's brain in three steps: first it grabs attention (Identification), then it builds connection (Story), and finally it plants the idea (Content). It works like telling a story where the viewer sees themselves, feels something and leaves with an idea in their head.

## Steps

### 1. Identification

Get inside the viewer's head until they think "it's like you're reading my mind" or "I've felt that too." Talk about routines, insecurities, wants, pop culture, feelings everyone has, in the audience's own words, like texting a friend. To do this, you need to know your audience well.

### 2. Story

A moment from your life or someone else's, with a beginning, middle and end, and how it hit you. It needs conflict (something went wrong), a turning point (what changed) and a consequence (what it taught). The story must be real: no invented experience.

**Example** (invented, to show the shape only):
"I posted over 100 videos and none of them got engagement. Then one day I realized I was trying to sound like someone I'm not. When I started writing from the heart, my content took off."

### 3. Content

The seed. An insight ("the problem was never you, it was the way you were taught"), a practical tip, a challenge or a hidden invitation to what you sell ("And that's why I teach script structure with so much passion inside my community.").

The content doesn't have to be technical: it has to matter to the bubble you are trying to break into. The moral of the story is the seed you want to plant in the viewer's head (see [commander's intent](../commanders-intent.md)).

What ISC asks for: the viewer thinks "that's me," the story has conflict, turning point and consequence, the seed is clear and the tone is conversational.

## Summary

- **I (Identification):** "You've been through this too."
- **S (Story):** "I lived this... or so-and-so lived this..."
- **C (Content):** "And what I learned from it was..."

## When to use it

- Building connection without sounding like you're "selling" something
- Placing your story inside a bigger message
- Teaching without sounding like a lecture
- Planting ideas and values through emotion

## Quick checklist

- **ST.identification-story-content.1.** Does the identification make the viewer think "that's me"?
- **ST.identification-story-content.2.** Does the story have conflict, a turning point and a consequence?
- **ST.identification-story-content.3.** Does the content plant one clear seed in the viewer's head?
- **ST.identification-story-content.4.** Is the tone conversational (nothing that sounds like a class)?
