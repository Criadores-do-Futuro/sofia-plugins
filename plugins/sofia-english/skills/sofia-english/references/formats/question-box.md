# Format: Question Box

Official id: `question-box`.

## What it is
An answer to a provocative question shown in Instagram's native question sticker (the "Ask me a question" box). The visible question is the premise and the hook. It is not a reply to a comment, and it is not just text typed over the video.

## Visual signature
- The native question sticker is visible, with the question written on it
- The creator reacts to and answers that specific question
- The question opens a debate, tension or identification

## Best-fit structure
[Wrong Way, Right Way](../structures/wrong-way-right-way.md) or [Them, Me, You, Future](../structures/them-me-you-future.md).

## Hook type this format asks for
- **FM.question-box.H.** A direct provocation: the box's question read out loud, followed by the creator's reaction.

## Dominant addictive elements
[Emotional relevance](../addictive-elements/emotional-relevance.md), [contrast](../addictive-elements/contrast.md), [theme and moral](../addictive-elements/theme-and-moral.md).

## Writing rules
- **FM.question-box.1.** Pick a question that touches a real belief, pain or split in the audience. Use a question the audience actually sent, quoted exactly; if you use an illustrative one, say so to the creator and never present it as sent by a real follower.
- **FM.question-box.2.** Show and read the question early; it has to make sense with no extra context.
- **FM.question-box.3.** React before giving the answer, to build anticipation.
- **FM.question-box.4.** Take a specific position, backed by a case, reasoning or contrast.
- **FM.question-box.5.** Make the answer useful to people who didn't send the question too.
- **FM.question-box.6.** Close by inviting new questions or asking the viewer to take a side.

## Don't confuse with
- Replying to a comment: that is not the Question Box.
- A question written as ordinary on-screen text: it can be [Direct to Camera](direct-to-camera.md) or a hot take, but not this format.
- A neutral question with no debate potential: it uses the interface but doesn't execute the format well.
