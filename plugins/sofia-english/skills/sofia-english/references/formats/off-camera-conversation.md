# Format: Off-Camera Conversation

Also called "bar talk".

## What it is
The person mostly speaks without looking at the lens, as if talking to someone sitting next to them. The averted gaze, relaxed posture and simple editing drop the "host mode" and create spontaneous intimacy.

## Visual signature
- Gaze off the lens for most of the video
- Relaxed posture and gestures
- Few changes of position or setting
- An informal tone, with no teleprompter performance

## Best-fit structure
[Them, Me, You, Future](../structures/them-me-you-future.md) or a confessional [ISC](../structures/identification-story-content.md).

## Hook type this format asks for
- **FM.off-camera-conversation.H.** A belief shock said as a confession ("It took me ten years to admit that {belief} was wrong.").

## Dominant addictive elements
[Familiar language](../addictive-elements/familiar-language.md), [emotional relevance](../addictive-elements/emotional-relevance.md), [theme and moral](../addictive-elements/theme-and-moral.md).

## Writing rules
- **FM.off-camera-conversation.1.** Write as speech aimed at one specific person, not an audience. Keep one clear listener.
- **FM.off-camera-conversation.2.** Open in the middle of a thought, a question or a confession.
- **FM.off-camera-conversation.3.** Use personal examples, interruptions and questions sparingly. The Floor still applies: no rhetorical questions in the spoken script.
- **FM.off-camera-conversation.4.** Avoid rigid lists, spoken titles and a lecture tone.
- **FM.off-camera-conversation.5.** Keep a clear line of reasoning under the naturalness, without pretending an unscripted interview took place.
- **FM.off-camera-conversation.6.** Close like the end of a conversation, not like an ad slogan.

## Don't confuse with
- A side teleprompter: a mechanical averted gaze, with no spontaneity.
- [Everyday Conversation](everyday-conversation.md): defined by the everyday setting, not the direction of the gaze.
- [Fast-Paced](fast-paced.md): frequent cuts and position changes break the feeling of one continuous conversation.
