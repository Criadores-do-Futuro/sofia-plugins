# Format: Lo-Fi

## What it is
A deliberately simple, raw, unpolished video. The feeling of spontaneity comes from a phone camera, available light, few cuts and imperfections left in. Lo-Fi doesn't mean careless: low production is a choice to increase closeness and credibility.

## Visual signature
- Natural light and surroundings, nothing that looks like a set
- Simple framing, handheld or propped on whatever is around
- Few cuts, fixes or graphic elements
- Small hesitations or background noise kept, as long as they don't get in the way of understanding

## Best-fit structure
A direct [ISC](../structures/identification-story-content.md), a hot take or a short story.

## Hook type this format asks for
- **FM.lo-fi.H.** A belief shock or a direct provocation: the strong opinion said in the first sentence, with no setup.

## Dominant addictive elements
[Familiar language](../addictive-elements/familiar-language.md), [emotional relevance](../addictive-elements/emotional-relevance.md), [conflict and change](../addictive-elements/conflict-and-change.md).

## Writing rules
- **FM.lo-fi.1.** Start fast, as if the idea just hit you.
- **FM.lo-fi.2.** Use a thesis strong enough to carry the lack of polished production.
- **FM.lo-fi.3.** Write for natural speech: short sentences, contractions and pauses.
- **FM.lo-fi.4.** Avoid formal intros, too many lists and text that sounds read off a teleprompter.
- **FM.lo-fi.5.** Keep the imperfections that feel human, but cut repetitions that add nothing.
- **FM.lo-fi.6.** Don't try to make up for the simplicity with heavy editing effects.

Audible speech, a readable image and a concrete message come first; roughness alone is not authenticity.

## Don't confuse with
- [Everyday Conversation](everyday-conversation.md): needs a recognizable everyday setting or object; Lo-Fi can happen anywhere.
- [Lazy Creative](lazy-creative.md): puts an elaborate visual hook at the start; Lo-Fi stays consistently simple.
- A poorly executed video: Lo-Fi keeps clear audio, intention and point of view.
