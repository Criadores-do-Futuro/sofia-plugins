# Format: Split Screen

This is the **recording format** axis (how the video is shot). Don't mix it up with the **structure** (the order of the argument, in [structures](../structures/README.md)) or the **archetype** (the angle, in [archetypes, niches and bubbles](../knowledge/archetypes-niches-bubbles.md)). A script has all three.

## What it is
The creator narrates or comments on outside content (someone else's video, a real scene) while that content appears beside or behind them.

## Visual signature
- The screen split in two: outside content on one side (or on top), the creator reacting on the other
- The outside content belongs to someone else: a video, a post, a real scene, a news story
- The creator pauses, points at or comments on parts of the outside content
- Sometimes the platform's native Duet (TikTok) or Remix (Instagram) feature appears

## Best-fit structure
[Them, Me, You, Future](../structures/them-me-you-future.md) + [contrast](../addictive-elements/contrast.md).

## Hook type this format asks for
- **FM.split-screen.H.** A curious statement of consequence or an immersive scene in the second person.

## Dominant addictive elements
[Emotional relevance](../addictive-elements/emotional-relevance.md), [contrast](../addictive-elements/contrast.md), [familiar language](../addictive-elements/familiar-language.md), [curiosity and aha](../addictive-elements/curiosity-and-aha.md).

## Writing rules
- **FM.split-screen.1.** Open with the consequence, not the context.
- **FM.split-screen.2.** Use "but" as a deliberate contrast device.
- **FM.split-screen.3.** Put the viewer inside the scene with "you" and "your".
- **FM.split-screen.4.** Each stretch raises the conflict of the one before.
- **FM.split-screen.5.** The aha is the loop's payoff: never reveal it before building tension.
- **FM.split-screen.6.** The moral turns the camera toward the viewer.
- **FM.split-screen.7.** Use real, verifiable brands, shows and names. Pick a specific moment of the reference, keep its context and separate what you observed from your interpretation.
- **FM.split-screen.8.** The CTA is the natural consequence of the moral.

## Don't confuse with
- [Comparison](comparison.md): both sides are options being compared; in Split Screen one side is the content being commented on and the other is the creator.
- [Green Screen](green-screen.md): the outside content appears BEHIND the creator, as a background; in Split Screen it shares the frame.
- [Analysis](analysis.md): interprets a subject with inserts cut in; if the outside content stays on screen with the creator, it's Split Screen.
