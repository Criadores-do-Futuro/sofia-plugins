# Format: Comparison

This is the **recording format** axis (how the video is shot). Don't mix it up with the **structure** (the order of the argument, in [structures](../structures/README.md)) or the **archetype** (the angle, in [archetypes, niches and bubbles](../knowledge/archetypes-niches-bubbles.md)). A script has all three.

## What it is
The creator compares two options, approaches, people or situations in a structured way, showing which one is better (and why) based on evidence.

## Visual signature
- Two options side by side or one after the other: two products, two ways, before and after
- Visual marks of opposition: a split screen, ✗ and ✓, a different color for each side
- The criteria appear one at a time, as on-screen text or spoken
- A clear verdict at the end, said or shown

## Best-fit structure
[Analysis](../structures/analysis.md) + [contrast](../addictive-elements/contrast.md).

## Hook type this format asks for
- **FM.comparison.H.** An unexpected contrast that sets the two sides against each other in the first sentence, in a way that surprises.

## Dominant addictive elements
[Contrast](../addictive-elements/contrast.md), [curiosity and aha](../addictive-elements/curiosity-and-aha.md), [theme and moral](../addictive-elements/theme-and-moral.md).

## Writing rules
- **FM.comparison.1.** Present both sides fairly before revealing the conclusion.
- **FM.comparison.2.** Use clear, verifiable criteria, the same ones for both sides.
- **FM.comparison.3.** Each criterion raises the contrast, from the most superficial to the deepest.
- **FM.comparison.4.** Never reveal which one is "the best" before building the full comparison.
- **FM.comparison.5.** Use real examples for each side: don't compare in the abstract. Any number in the comparison needs a source.
- **FM.comparison.6.** The story's turn is the deciding criterion that changes everything.
- **FM.comparison.7.** The moral is the lesson that comes out of the comparison, not the creator's personal preference.
- **FM.comparison.8.** The CTA invites the viewer to apply the deciding criterion to their own life.

## Don't confuse with
- [Split Screen](split-screen.md): the split shows outside content being commented on; in Comparison both sides are the options being compared.
- [Analysis](analysis.md): interprets a single subject; Comparison needs two sides and criteria.
- [Wrong Way, Right Way](../structures/wrong-way-right-way.md) is a script structure, not a format: it can be shot as [Direct to Camera](direct-to-camera.md) without becoming a Comparison.
