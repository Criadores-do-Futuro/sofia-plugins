# Format: Social Experiment

This is the **recording format** axis (how the video is shot). Don't mix it up with the **structure** (the order of the argument, in [structures](../structures/README.md)) or the **archetype** (the angle, in [archetypes, niches and bubbles](../knowledge/archetypes-niches-bubbles.md)). A script has all three.

## What it is
The creator runs or documents a real experiment with real people and reveals the results to the viewer. The premise is the hook.

## Visual signature
- Real people, outside the creator's circle, reacting to a set-up situation
- A hidden or distant camera, sometimes with several people tested in a row
- The premise announced at the start, in text or speech, and the result revealed at the end
- A public setting: street, store, public transit, event

## Best-fit structure
[ISC](../structures/identification-story-content.md), opening on the premise as the hook.

## Hook type this format asks for
- **FM.social-experiment.H.** A statement of the experiment's premise that creates immediate anticipation ("I did {X} with {Y} people to find out {Z}.").

## Dominant addictive elements
[Curiosity and aha](../addictive-elements/curiosity-and-aha.md), [emotional relevance](../addictive-elements/emotional-relevance.md), [theme and moral](../addictive-elements/theme-and-moral.md).

## Writing rules
- **FM.social-experiment.1.** The experiment's premise is the hook: direct and specific.
- **FM.social-experiment.2.** Explain the experiment's "rules" in 1 or 2 sentences, no more.
- **FM.social-experiment.3.** Document the reactions impartially: let the viewer draw conclusions before the creator does.
- **FM.social-experiment.4.** Build suspense before revealing the result.
- **FM.social-experiment.5.** The aha is the experiment's result: save it for the moment of highest tension.
- **FM.social-experiment.6.** The moral comes out of the results; the creator doesn't impose it.
- **FM.social-experiment.7.** Use real, identifiable people: anonymity weakens the impact. Get consent where it's needed before showing anyone.
- **FM.social-experiment.8.** The CTA connects the experiment's result to a personal reflection for the viewer.

Use an actual setup and the outcomes you observed. Never fabricate participants, reactions or results; the script can't be written before the footage exists, only the premise and the frame around it.

## Don't confuse with
- [Situational Conflict](situational-conflict.md): the situation looks real but is staged by actors; in a Social Experiment the reactions come from people who don't know.
- [Vlog](vlog.md): records what happens; a Social Experiment sets up a situation to test a hypothesis.
- A street interview with no hypothesis being tested is not a Social Experiment.
