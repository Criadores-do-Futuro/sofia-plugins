# Format: Analysis

This is the **recording format** axis (how the video is shot). Don't mix it up with the **structure** (the order of the argument, in [structures](../structures/README.md)) or the **archetype** (the angle, in [archetypes, niches and bubbles](../knowledge/archetypes-niches-bubbles.md)). A script has all three.

## What it is
The creator interprets a recognizable person, brand, strategy, trend or character. There is visual material of the subject, and the spoken lines reveal a cause, pattern or meaning instead of just describing or reacting. The subject delivers the entertainment (the punchlines come out of their mouth, in short clips), the presenter names the mechanism, and the viewer gets the lesson.

## Visual signature
- Opens on a clip of the subject, with no introduction first
- Clips of the subject in a card over the presenter (with the clip's audio) or in a mask behind them (muted, under the presenter's voice); never full screen
- Screenshots of the profile and numbers (followers, views) appear at the moment they are said
- An on-screen title for each principle ("1. [principle name]")
- The presenter's captions in one style, the subject's lines in another

## Best-fit structure
[Analysis](../structures/analysis.md) (Conflict, Results, 3 Reasons, Moral). Alternative: [AIDA](../structures/aida.md).

## Hook type this format asks for
- **FM.analysis.H.** A clip of the subject, 4 seconds at most, that makes sense on its own and ends on the punchline. Right after it, an exaggerated label about something harmless about them (a comic fake headline, "the most {Y} {X} on the internet" or an affectionate insult), turned into praise in the next sentence. The Portuguese originals pivot with "the truth is"; in English, pivot with a plain statement ("And {name} is the best at {Y} on the internet"), because [the Floor](../floor.md) cuts throat-clearing openers.

## Dominant addictive elements
[Curiosity and aha](../addictive-elements/curiosity-and-aha.md), [contrast](../addictive-elements/contrast.md), [emotional relevance](../addictive-elements/emotional-relevance.md).

## Writing rules
- **FM.analysis.1.** Hook = a clip of the subject (4 seconds or less, self-contained, punchline at the end). Without a clip like that, pick a different subject.
- **FM.analysis.2.** Credential before the promise: rounded follower count, average or peak views, and one unique detail. Every number comes from a source you opened.
- **FM.analysis.3.** The promise lands between 19 and 26 seconds, with the subject as the grammatical subject: "So today we're breaking down the three principles {name} uses to...".
- **FM.analysis.4.** Three principles, each as name, then proof (a list of 3 with an anticlimax, plus a clip that shows it on its own), then mechanism, then an imperative lesson for "you", then a bridge to the next.
- **FM.analysis.5.** At least one principle is named after something from the subject themself; the last principle is the pillar of the presenter's own method or product.
- **FM.analysis.6.** Separate verifiable fact (numbers, clips) from interpretation ("what I call...").
- **FM.analysis.7.** The presenter only talks about themself in the close; before the CTA, turn to the viewer (a mirror question, a call-out or a dramatized objection).
- **FM.analysis.8.** CTA with a callback to the subject: "too bad {name} doesn't teach {X}... but I do".
- **FM.analysis.9.** The label and the jokes never insult the creator's own audience; profanity only comes from the subject's mouth.

The subject is always someone else who entertains, never the creator's own profile.

## Don't confuse with
- Reaction: emotion without interpretation is not Analysis.
- Opinion vlog: talking about something without visual material of the subject doesn't execute this format.
- [Green Screen](green-screen.md) and [Split Screen](split-screen.md): visual techniques; they only become Analysis when there is an analytical build.
- A lesson about a concept using your own videos as examples: without a subject who entertains, it becomes a lecture (and loses the entertainment).
