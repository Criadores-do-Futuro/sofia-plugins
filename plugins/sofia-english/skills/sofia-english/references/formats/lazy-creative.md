# Format: Lazy Creative

A strong visual opening followed by a simple body.

## What it is
All the visual energy sits in the first 3 to 6 seconds: an odd angle, an unexpected close-up, an intriguing object or an out-of-pattern situation. After that, the video switches to simple filming, usually the person talking to camera.

## Visual signature
- An immediate break in the visual pattern
- A clear contrast between the elaborate opening and the simple rest
- A hook carried by framing, action or an object, not only by the line

## Best-fit structure
[AIDA](../structures/aida.md) or a short [ISC](../structures/identification-story-content.md).

## Hook type this format asks for
- **FM.lazy-creative.H.** An unexpected visual contrast in the first 3 to 6 seconds, with a line that only makes sense together with the image.

## Dominant addictive elements
[Curiosity and aha](../addictive-elements/curiosity-and-aha.md), [contrast](../addictive-elements/contrast.md).

## Writing rules
- **FM.lazy-creative.1.** Make the first line depend on what appears in the opening.
- **FM.lazy-creative.2.** Create a visual question that the spoken line promises to answer.
- **FM.lazy-creative.3.** Move quickly to the simple framing without it feeling like a different video.
- **FM.lazy-creative.4.** Use the rest to pay off the curiosity the hook created. The image must be relevant, and the body must keep its promise.
- **FM.lazy-creative.5.** Don't keep the same level of stimulation for the whole video; the contrast defines the format.
- **FM.lazy-creative.6.** Bring back the opening element in the payoff when that strengthens the whole.

## Don't confuse with
- [Fast-Paced](fast-paced.md): keeps position changes and stimulation going for the whole video.
- [Lo-Fi](lo-fi.md): simple all the way through, with no required standout visual opening.
- Eye-catching text over an ordinary image: the hook has to be visual, not just typographic or verbal.
