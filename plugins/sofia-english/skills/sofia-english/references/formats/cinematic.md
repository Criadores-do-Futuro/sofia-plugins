# Format: Cinematic

This is the **recording format** axis (how the video is shot). Don't mix it up with the **structure** (the order of the argument, in [structures](../structures/README.md)) or the **archetype** (the angle, in [archetypes, niches and bubbles](../knowledge/archetypes-niches-bubbles.md)). A script has all three.

## What it is
A video with a film look: well-composed shots, a soundtrack, scripted narration or dialogue, precise cuts. The content feels like a short film.

## Visual signature
- Composed, varied shots (wide, detail, reverse shot), sometimes in a cinema aspect ratio
- The soundtrack carries the emotion; little or no talking straight to camera
- Cuts timed to the music or the action, not to a spoken sentence
- Graded color, deliberate lighting, a stable camera or controlled movement

## Best-fit structure
[ISC](../structures/identification-story-content.md), opening on an immersive scene.

## Hook type this format asks for
- **FM.cinematic.H.** An immersive scene that puts the viewer inside a specific moment before any context.

## Dominant addictive elements
[Emotional relevance](../addictive-elements/emotional-relevance.md), [conflict and change](../addictive-elements/conflict-and-change.md), [theme and moral](../addictive-elements/theme-and-moral.md).

## Writing rules
- **FM.cinematic.1.** Open in medias res: inside the scene, not before it.
- **FM.cinematic.2.** Every line of the script matches a specific scene or shot. Write actions that can actually be filmed, and let the images carry information instead of narrating everything.
- **FM.cinematic.3.** Show the conflict; don't explain it.
- **FM.cinematic.4.** Use dialogue or inner narration to reveal the character's emotional state.
- **FM.cinematic.5.** The turn is a clear visual moment, not just a line.
- **FM.cinematic.6.** The moral is never said out loud: it comes out of the last scene.
- **FM.cinematic.7.** The rhythm of the text mirrors the rhythm of the cut (short sentences = fast cuts).
- **FM.cinematic.8.** The CTA, if there is one, fits naturally into the end of the scene.

## Don't confuse with
- [Visual Storytelling](visual-storytelling.md): tells the story with on-screen text and graphics; Cinematic tells it with shots and acting.
- [Fast-Paced](fast-paced.md): also cuts a lot, but for energy; Cinematic cuts to build scene and emotion.
- A well-shot [Vlog](vlog.md): records what happened; Cinematic stages a scripted moment.
