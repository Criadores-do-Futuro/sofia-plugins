# Format: Green Screen

This is the **recording format** axis (how the video is shot). Don't mix it up with the **structure** (the order of the argument, in [structures](../structures/README.md)) or the **archetype** (the angle, in [archetypes, niches and bubbles](../knowledge/archetypes-niches-bubbles.md)). A script has all three.

## What it is
The creator appears in front of a background replaced by an image, screenshot, chart or real scene. The background is an active part of the story.

## Visual signature
- The creator cut out in front of a replaced background: a screenshot, post, chart, website or scene
- The background changes as the argument moves forward
- The creator points at the background or stands beside what they're commenting on
- Usually a medium or close shot, with the creator taking up part of the frame

## Best-fit structure
[Analysis](../structures/analysis.md) + [ISC](../structures/identification-story-content.md).

## Hook type this format asks for
- **FM.green-screen.H.** A statement of consequence that points straight at the visual in the background.

## Dominant addictive elements
[Curiosity and aha](../addictive-elements/curiosity-and-aha.md), [contrast](../addictive-elements/contrast.md), [emotional relevance](../addictive-elements/emotional-relevance.md).

## Writing rules
- **FM.green-screen.1.** The hook mentions or points straight at the background visual.
- **FM.green-screen.2.** Build every sentence as if the visual were proof of what's being said.
- **FM.green-screen.3.** Use real data, verifiable screenshots and recognizable names. The reference has to be legible, and the creator must not cover the essential detail.
- **FM.green-screen.4.** Build suspense before revealing the aha.
- **FM.green-screen.5.** The story's turn is the moment the visual and the spoken line line up.
- **FM.green-screen.6.** The moral comes from what the visual proves, not from the creator's opinion.
- **FM.green-screen.7.** Use analytical but accessible language, like a friend explaining something they found out.
- **FM.green-screen.8.** The CTA connects the visual insight to a specific action for the viewer.

## Don't confuse with
- [Split Screen](split-screen.md): the outside content shares the frame with the creator; in Green Screen it's the background.
- [Visual Storytelling](visual-storytelling.md): the graphics tell the story on their own; in Green Screen the creator is always on screen commenting.
- [Direct to Camera](direct-to-camera.md): if the background is the real room and carries no content, it's Direct to Camera.
