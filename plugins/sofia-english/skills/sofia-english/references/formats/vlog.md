# Format: Vlog

## What it is
A record of a slice of life as it happens: a routine, a trip somewhere, a behind-the-scenes moment or a process. Real moments in different settings form a story with a beginning, middle and end, even if it's short.

## Visual signature
- Two or more distinct moments, activities or settings
- A noticeable passage of time or change of place
- First-person narration connecting what happens
- A real experience being lived, not just told afterward in front of the camera

## Best-fit structure
[ISC](../structures/identification-story-content.md) or [Them, Me, You, Future](../structures/them-me-you-future.md).

## Hook type this format asks for
- **FM.vlog.H.** An immersive scene from the vlog's strongest moment, or a statement of consequence ("I went to {place} thinking {expectation}. I came back {change}.").

## Dominant addictive elements
[Conflict and change](../addictive-elements/conflict-and-change.md), [curiosity and aha](../addictive-elements/curiosity-and-aha.md), [emotional relevance](../addictive-elements/emotional-relevance.md).

## Writing rules
- **FM.vlog.1.** Define the journey's promise: what's going to happen, be decided or be discovered.
- **FM.vlog.2.** Narrate the transitions the image can't explain on its own, without describing the obvious.
- **FM.vlog.3.** Pick the moments that change the state of the story; don't catalog everything that happened.
- **FM.vlog.4.** Include an obstacle, a surprise or a decision to create progression.
- **FM.vlog.5.** Alternate on-camera presence, surroundings and details to give the experience texture.
- **FM.vlog.6.** End by showing the result and what changed for the creator.

Use events that actually happened and real cause-and-effect transitions. Never pass off an invented day or a reconstructed outcome as real.

## Don't confuse with
- [Everyday Conversation](everyday-conversation.md): usually stays in a single everyday setting, with no journey.
- [Fast-Paced](fast-paced.md): changes position for rhythm, but doesn't necessarily record a real experience.
- [Voice-Over](voice-over.md): can use lots of B-roll, but doesn't require it to be stages of a lived experience with an arc.
