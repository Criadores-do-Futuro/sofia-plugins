# Format: Behind the Scenes

This is the **recording format** axis (how the video is shot). Don't mix it up with the **structure** (the order of the argument, in [structures](../structures/README.md)) or the **archetype** (the angle, in [archetypes, niches and bubbles](../knowledge/archetypes-niches-bubbles.md)). A script has all three.

## What it is
A video that shows the real process behind a result, a creation or a routine. It creates a sense of exclusive access and authenticity.

## Visual signature
- The work happening on camera: hands, tools, screen, workbench or set in use
- The final result shows up at the start or the end, and the middle is the process
- A real workspace, with the mess and the tools in view
- Direct talk or narration explaining what's being done while it's done

## Best-fit structure
[ISC](../structures/identification-story-content.md), opening on the result before the process.

## Hook type this format asks for
- **FM.behind-the-scenes.H.** A statement of the surprising result before revealing the process ("I made {X} in {Y time}. Here's the part nobody shows.").

## Dominant addictive elements
[Curiosity and aha](../addictive-elements/curiosity-and-aha.md), [emotional relevance](../addictive-elements/emotional-relevance.md), [conflict and change](../addictive-elements/conflict-and-change.md).

## Writing rules
- **FM.behind-the-scenes.1.** Open with the result, never with the process.
- **FM.behind-the-scenes.2.** Use the script to build the process as a sequence of discoveries.
- **FM.behind-the-scenes.3.** Include the real mistakes and obstacles: behind the scenes with no friction isn't authentic. Show the actual steps; never reconstruct a process that didn't happen.
- **FM.behind-the-scenes.4.** Use casual, intimate language, as if you were showing a friend.
- **FM.behind-the-scenes.5.** The story's turn is the moment something went unexpectedly wrong (or unexpectedly right).
- **FM.behind-the-scenes.6.** The aha is the reveal of what really mattered in the process.
- **FM.behind-the-scenes.7.** The moral is a lesson learned in the process, not a theory brought in beforehand.
- **FM.behind-the-scenes.8.** The CTA invites the viewer to try the same process or to follow for more behind the scenes.

## Don't confuse with
- [Vlog](vlog.md): records a day or an experience; Behind the Scenes records the process of ONE specific result.
- [Lo-Fi](lo-fi.md): is about raw production; Behind the Scenes can be well produced. What defines it is showing the process.
- [Green Screen](green-screen.md): if the process only appears as a screenshot behind the creator, it's Green Screen, not Behind the Scenes.
