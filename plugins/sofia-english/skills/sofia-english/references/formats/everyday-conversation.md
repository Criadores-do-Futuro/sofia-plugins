# Format: Everyday Conversation

## What it is
The person talks in an everyday, intimate setting, surrounded by recognizable things from daily life: coffee, food, makeup, dishes, clothes or kitchen tools. Doing something helps but isn't required; the setting and the objects already create the feeling of a video call with a friend.

## Visual signature
- A home or intimate setting
- An everyday object visible or in hand
- Simple production and a conversational tone

## Best-fit structure
[Them, Me, You, Future](../structures/them-me-you-future.md) or a short [ISC](../structures/identification-story-content.md).

## Hook type this format asks for
- **FM.everyday-conversation.H.** A belief shock said as a confession, with the object in hand.

## Dominant addictive elements
[Familiar language](../addictive-elements/familiar-language.md), [emotional relevance](../addictive-elements/emotional-relevance.md), [theme and moral](../addictive-elements/theme-and-moral.md).

## Writing rules
- **FM.everyday-conversation.1.** Write it as conversation running alongside the activity, not as a formal presentation.
- **FM.everyday-conversation.2.** Open with an intimate observation, a confession or a line someone would say in the kitchen.
- **FM.everyday-conversation.3.** Use the object or activity when it can reinforce the idea, without turning everything into a tutorial.
- **FM.everyday-conversation.4.** Use short sentences, pauses and small natural tangents.
- **FM.everyday-conversation.5.** Keep one clear central point so the spontaneity doesn't turn into rambling. Keep the situation specific and let a meaningful turn come up naturally.
- **FM.everyday-conversation.6.** Close with a simple conclusion, like someone finishing a thought out loud.

## Don't confuse with
- [Vlog](vlog.md): needs a sequence of moments or settings and the story of an experience.
- Tutorial: the focus is teaching the activity or technique.
- [Off-Camera Conversation](off-camera-conversation.md): defined mainly by the gaze off the lens; Everyday Conversation is defined by the everyday setting.
