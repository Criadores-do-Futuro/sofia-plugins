# Format: Fast-Paced

This is the **recording format** axis (how the video is shot). Don't mix it up with the **structure** (the order of the argument, in [structures](../structures/README.md)) or the **archetype** (the angle, in [archetypes, niches and bubbles](../knowledge/archetypes-niches-bubbles.md)). A script has all three.

## What it is
A video with quick cuts, camera movement, changing framing and a fast pace. The visual energy carries retention (how long people keep watching).

## Visual signature
- Cuts every 1 to 2 seconds, zooms, changes of framing and setting
- Lots of graphic elements: animated captions, emojis, sound effects, short B-roll
- Fast talking with no pauses, often with jump cuts
- Little time spent still on the same shot

## Best-fit structure
[AIDA](../structures/aida.md) or [Analysis](../structures/analysis.md) (as a list).

## Hook type this format asks for
- **FM.fast-paced.H.** A direct one-sentence statement of the benefit or result, followed by an immediate cut to the first point.

## Dominant addictive elements
[Curiosity and aha](../addictive-elements/curiosity-and-aha.md), [familiar language](../addictive-elements/familiar-language.md), [conflict and change](../addictive-elements/conflict-and-change.md).

## Writing rules
- **FM.fast-paced.1.** Each sentence matches a cut or camera move: write short. Every cut adds information; keep on-screen text readable.
- **FM.fast-paced.2.** Use implied numbered lists: the viewer should feel progress.
- **FM.fast-paced.3.** Direct, to-the-point language, with no long intros.
- **FM.fast-paced.4.** Each point is more surprising or valuable than the one before.
- **FM.fast-paced.5.** Vary the kind of information: statistic → example → question → statement. A statistic needs a source; a question follows the Floor (only the CTA question or one that came from a comment).
- **FM.fast-paced.6.** Avoid long linking phrases: let the cuts do the transitions.
- **FM.fast-paced.7.** The aha is the strongest point: place it at the rhythm's peak.
- **FM.fast-paced.8.** Deliver the CTA at peak energy: don't slow down before the end.

Speed alone doesn't guarantee retention.

## Don't confuse with
- [Cinematic](cinematic.md): cuts to build a scene; Fast-Paced cuts to keep the energy high.
- [Direct to Camera](direct-to-camera.md) with fast editing: if the cuts only remove pauses and the shot never changes, it's Direct to Camera.
- [Lazy Creative](lazy-creative.md): the energy is only in the hook; in Fast-Paced it lasts until the end.
