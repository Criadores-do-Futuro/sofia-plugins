# Format: The Office

A mockumentary, named after the show.

This is the **recording format** axis (how the video is shot). Don't mix it up with the **structure** (the order of the argument, in [structures](../structures/README.md)) or the **archetype** (the angle, in [archetypes, niches and bubbles](../knowledge/archetypes-niches-bubbles.md)). A script has all three.

## What it is
A video with a mockumentary look: a fixed or slightly shaky camera, the creator looking at the camera as if in a documentary interview, a tone somewhere between serious and ironic.

## Visual signature
- A direct look at the camera in the middle of a scene, like a documentary interview
- Cuts between the scene happening and the confessional talking head
- A fixed or slightly shaky camera, mockumentary style
- Humor through silent reaction: the look at the camera says what the line doesn't

## Best-fit structure
[ISC](../structures/identification-story-content.md) with inner narration (the look at the camera as a confession).

## Hook type this format asks for
- **FM.the-office.H.** A direct statement to camera that creates intimacy and weirdness at the same time.

## Dominant addictive elements
[Familiar language](../addictive-elements/familiar-language.md), [emotional relevance](../addictive-elements/emotional-relevance.md), [theme and moral](../addictive-elements/theme-and-moral.md) (with irony).

## Writing rules
- **FM.the-office.1.** The creator talks as if being filmed without knowing it, in a confessional tone.
- **FM.the-office.2.** Use looks at the camera as "commentary" on what's happening, each with a purpose.
- **FM.the-office.3.** The humor comes from the contrast between what's said and what's obvious.
- **FM.the-office.4.** Keep the irony subtle: never explain the joke.
- **FM.the-office.5.** The situation is recognizable and universally awkward or true. Keep the character's intention coherent.
- **FM.the-office.6.** The moral comes out as a confession, not a lesson.
- **FM.the-office.7.** Use strategic pauses and silences: the rhythm is what makes it funny.
- **FM.the-office.8.** Deliver the CTA in the same confessional tone as the rest of the video.

It is staged: say so when a factual reading could mislead the viewer.

## Don't confuse with
- [Dialogue](dialogue.md): the characters talk to each other without breaking the fourth wall; The Office breaks it on purpose.
- [Direct to Camera](direct-to-camera.md): talks to the camera as a presentation; The Office talks as a character in a confessional.
- [Narrator](narrator.md): the off-screen voice comments on the character; in The Office the character comments on the scene.
