# Format: Combined Format

## What it is
Two recognizable formats are fused on purpose into one coherent language with its own personality. Adding a one-off effect isn't enough: each format has to contribute to the structure.

## Visual signature
- You can name both formats present
- Each layer keeps its recognizable traits
- The switch, or the overlap, feels intentional
- The fusion adds rhythm, meaning or point of view

## Best-fit structure
Depends on the two formats. Pick one dominant narrative structure (see [structures](../structures/README.md)) and adapt the visual rules of both formats.

## Hook type this format asks for
- **FM.combined-format.H.** The hook of the leading format. When the mix itself is the surprise (for example, a [Direct to Camera](direct-to-camera.md) that turns [Cinematic](cinematic.md) mid-sentence), use an unexpected contrast.

## Dominant addictive elements
Those of the leading format, plus [contrast](../addictive-elements/contrast.md) when the mix creates contrast.

## Writing rules
- **FM.combined-format.1.** Decide internally which format leads the story and which one extends the execution.
- **FM.combined-format.2.** Give each format a different job; don't duplicate the same effect.
- **FM.combined-format.3.** Plan where each layer comes in, leaves or overlaps.
- **FM.combined-format.4.** Keep the minimum signals that make each format recognizable.
- **FM.combined-format.5.** Make the transitions carry meaning, not just variety. Keep continuity when switching.
- **FM.combined-format.6.** Review the script against the rules of both formats and resolve conflicts on purpose.
- **FM.combined-format.7.** When analyzing, name the combination as `Format A + Format B`.

## Don't confuse with
- One format with a change of setting or a brief insert is still a single format.
- A chaotic mix without two recognizable patterns is not a combination.
- A second format that shows up for a few seconds with no structural job is just a one-off device.
