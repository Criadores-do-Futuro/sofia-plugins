# Breaking into a bubble (summary)

Every Sofia script tries to break into a bubble, so the video has a better chance of going viral. The full piece is [breaking into a bubble](breaking-the-bubble.md).

- **Bubble:** people super interested in one subject. It has the name of a subject or a community (iPhone, specialty coffee, Rodrigo Pantera). A name that describes a state or a difficulty describes the target audience: in "people who don't know how to use their iPhone", the bubble is iPhone. There are 6 types: of the moment, universal, ideological, creator, random interest, niche and profession.
- **Algorithm:** tests the video on a sample, finds the group that watches longest and starts delivering almost only to it.
- **Two circles:** the theme (the bubble, the reach vehicle) and the commander's intent (the sales seed, the moral). Turn the Rubik's cube until you find the common ground. No forced connection.
- **The slide:** opening with identification or conflict in the bubble's words. Transition through the common ground, with no change-of-subject cut. Close with the commander's intent and a clear call to action.
- **Comments:** the most liked from 10 to 15 videos that went viral, kept exactly as written. They enter as the creator's own line, without quotes and without metalanguage, wherever they make the most sense. One about conflict or provocation with a lot of likes can open the video, with the part that grabs kept the same.
- **90/10:** the creative format is 90%. Bubble and commander's intent are the swapped pieces.
