# Idea template

Every video idea Sofia proposes comes out in this template. It forces you to think through the whole video before writing: how it's recorded, the order of the story and what hooks the viewer in each part.

## Before filling it in

1. Read the format piece ([formats](formats/README.md) to choose, then the format's own file). Check its rule (in Analysis, for example, the analyzed subject is always someone outside the creator's profile).
2. Read the structure the format points to ([structures](structures/README.md)).
3. Read the hierarchy of the elements ([addictive elements](addictive-elements/README.md)) and the format's dominant elements.
4. Look at what performed above the profile's normal. An idea that starts from a winner is its next version: the same mechanism, with another subject.
5. Read [breaking the bubble](breaking-the-bubble.md) and [commander's intent](commanders-intent.md). Every idea tries to break into a bubble (the theme, first circle) and plants a seed of the creator's commander's intent (second circle), joined by a common ground.

## The template

```
### Idea {number}: {short title, the way the creator would say it}

Theme: {the subject that makes people stop}
Bubble: {the group that is super into the theme} ({bubble type})
Seed ({pillar}): {the idea that sticks, in one sentence; it is the video's moral}
Common ground: {what connects the theme to the seed, without forcing it}
CTA: {hidden, inside the story of ..., or direct}
Who it's for: {who the creator wants as a client}
Where it came from: {the above-normal video that inspires it, or "new subject"}

Format: {format name}, because {reason in one sentence}
Structure: {structure name}, because {reason in one sentence}

Blocks:
1. {block name}: {what happens, in one line}
2. {block name}: {what happens}
3. {block name}: {what happens}

Addictive elements:
- {element}: shows up in {block or second}, when {what happens there}
- {element}: shows up in {block or second}, when {what happens there}

Possible hook: {the spoken line, of the type the format asks for} / On-screen text: {3 to 5 words}
Needs to be real: {the case, number or source the creator needs to have}
```

## Rules

- **IT1.** **Bubble and seed, always.** The bubble is named after a subject or a community; a name that describes a state or a difficulty goes in "Who it's for". The seed is a single one, from one pillar (Authenticity, Movement or Product), and comes from the creator's notes or from what they told you. With no notes and no conversation about it, ask before inventing. In the video brief, the line "Seed ({pillar}): {the sentence}" goes in the Idea.
- **IT2.** **The three axes, always.** Format, structure and addictive elements, with the place where each element shows up. An element's name with no place doesn't count.
- **IT3.** **Format and structure are different things.** The format says how to record. The structure says the order of the story. One format can use more than one structure.
- **IT4.** **The elements follow the hierarchy.** Theme and moral first. An idea without a strong theme can't be saved with contrast.
- **IT5.** **An element happens in the scene.** In the video, nobody hears the element's name. Say what the viewer sees or hears at that point.
- **IT6.** **Facts with a source.** If the idea depends on a number or a case, mark it in "Needs to be real". Without a source, the idea changes or goes.
- **IT7.** **No invented decisions.** A low number doesn't mean the creator gave up on a theme. If the data conflicts with what they asked for, ask.
- **IT8.** **[The Floor](floor.md) applies here too**, including in the title and the possible hook.
- **IT9.** **A variation of what worked.** The idea starts from what performed above the profile's normal when there is a winner, and says where it came from.
- **IT10.** **The format's rule applies.** In Analysis, the analyzed subject is someone from outside who entertains, never the creator's own profile.

## Example

Invented example, only to show the shape.

```
### Idea 1: The bakery that doubled the price of bread and got busier

Theme: prices going up and customers who stay
Bubble: bakery and pastry shop owners (niche and profession)
Seed (Movement): if you sell too cheap, you're working for free
Common ground: the price of bread, which every bakery owner has been afraid to raise
CTA: hidden, in block 2, in the story of when the creator charged too little
Who it's for: small business owners who are afraid to raise prices
Where it came from: the video about the salon that charged too little hit 3x the profile's normal

Format: Visual Storytelling, because the old and new prices appear on screen as the story moves
Structure: Them, Me, You, Future, because it starts with his case and ends with the viewer's

Blocks:
1. Them: the bakery owner says he raised the price of bread and waited for the line to disappear
2. Me: the creator tells when they charged too little and paid to work
3. You: the math the viewer does for their own business
4. Future: what changes the month they charge the right price

Addictive elements:
- Emotional relevance: in the hook, with the fear of losing customers
- Conflict and change: in block 1, when the line grows instead of disappearing
- Curiosity and aha: opens in the hook and pays off at the end of block 1, with the reason for the line
- Familiar language: in block 3, with the math of bread and coffee

Possible hook: "The owner of this bakery doubled the price of bread and the line got longer." / On-screen text: "Doubled the price, got busier"
Needs to be real: the bakery case, with name, city and the old and new prices
```
