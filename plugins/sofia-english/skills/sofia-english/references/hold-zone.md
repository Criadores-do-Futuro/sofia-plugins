# The hold zone

The hook makes the viewer stop scrolling. The hold zone makes them stay. It is the 5 to 10 seconds right after the hook, 2 to 4 sentences, and it is where the viewer decides whether to watch to the end. This piece applies to the bridge after the hook, to the script and to the critique. The hook itself is in [how to make the hook](hook.md).

## 1. The two checks

Without noticing, someone who just stopped checks two things:

1. **Is the promise real?** The video is about the subject the hook promised and starts delivering the solution, without changing the topic. If the viewer feels they fell for bait, they leave.
2. **Can I trust the person talking?** They decide fast whether the person on screen is the right one for that subject and won't waste their time.

If either one fails, a good hook becomes just a video the viewer stopped for and dropped.

## 2. The four trust factors

### Concrete proof, the strongest

A study, a screenshot, a practical example or a real case, shown right at the start. In a video that teaches, proof weighs more than anything, because it shows the hook's promise has a basis.

- The proof is real and has a source: the study with its name and link, the actual screenshot, the case that happened. Invented proof stays out, always.
- Without sourced proof, a concrete example the creator lived or told works, said as theirs.
- In the video, the proof appears on screen (screenshot, chart, before and after) while the spoken line says what it shows.

### Identification

A real person, from the same world as the viewer, with the face you'd expect from someone who talks about that subject. For example, a younger creator talking about new trends, or a traditional figure talking about accounting and finance. In the script, identification shows up in everyday details of the bubble, in the bubble's own words ([breaking the bubble](breaking-the-bubble.md)).

### Presence

A confident posture that fits the subject: looking at the camera, a steady voice, no apologizing for being there.

### Production care

Good light, clean audio, the right framing and a sharp image. A sloppy video signals carelessness before the viewer hears the second sentence.

Proof and identification go into the script's text. Presence and production care depend on the recording: they become items on the recording checklist, not sentences in the script.

## 3. How to write the hold zone

Right after the hook's spoken line, 2 to 4 sentences that:

1. confirm the hook's subject, with the same words or close ones, without switching topics;
2. deliver a concrete piece of what was promised (step 6 of [the hook](hook.md), value in the next second);
3. show the proof or the concrete example;
4. when the structure saves the full answer for the end, make it clear the rest is coming.

Invented example, only to show the shape. The parts in braces come from real facts in the video brief.

```
Hook: "The owner of this bakery doubled the price of bread and the line got longer."
Hold zone: "Bread used to cost {old price}. Today it costs {new price}, and the line
{what you see at the door, in the screenshot or in the footage}. He changed one thing
along with the price, and that one thing is what keeps customers coming back."
```

## 4. How the hold zone talks to the rest of the method

- **The slide** ([breaking the bubble](breaking-the-bubble.md)): the hold zone is still part of the opening, in the bubble's words. It pays the hook's promise before or during the transition to the common ground.
- **Curiosity gap:** the full answer can wait until the end, but the hold zone delivers a concrete piece and shows the rest is coming.
- **Commander's intent** ([commander's intent](commanders-intent.md)): it belongs to the development and the close. The hold zone carries no promotion and no call to action.
- **Format:** the format of the video brief decides how the proof shows up. On green screen, the screenshot or chart becomes the background; in an analysis, the analyzed video itself is the proof.

## 5. In the critique

Check the seconds right after the hook:

- **HZ1.** The 2 to 4 sentences are about the same subject the hook promised.
- **HZ2.** A concrete piece of the promise arrives in those seconds.
- **HZ3.** There is real proof, with a source, or a concrete example from the creator.
- **HZ4.** The bubble recognizes itself: an everyday detail of its world, in its words.
- **HZ5.** No introduction, promotion or change of subject in this stretch.
- **HZ6.** Presence and production care are on the recording checklist.

Whatever fails becomes a highlight.
