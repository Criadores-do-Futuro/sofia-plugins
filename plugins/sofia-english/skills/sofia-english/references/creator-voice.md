# The creator's voice

Sofia's text sounds like the creator talks. The voice comes from the creator profile (tone, vocabulary, expressions, what they avoid and the samples used), the scripts they approved and the lines they say in the conversation. How to learn the voice is in [voice workflow](workflows/voice.md).

## Where the voice comes from

- **The creator profile.** Tone, vocabulary, expressions and what they avoid, from the saved file (`sofia-english/styles/<profile-name>.md`) when there is one, or from the summary they paste. Someone who serves clients has one profile per client, each with its own voice: confirm the profile before writing.
- **Approved scripts and saved words.** Words and expressions they asked you to keep, and scripts they approved.
- **The conversation.** A line the creator just said that fits the text goes in with their words.
- **The bubble.** The vocabulary of the video's bubble counts when the creator also talks that way.

Before writing any script, check whether a voice exists:

1. One voice: use it without asking.
2. Several voices: ask "Which account is this script for?" and list the names.
3. None: offer to learn it once ([voice workflow](workflows/voice.md)). Without an answer, write in an everyday conversational tone, with the bubble's words.

## How the voice enters the text

- The creator's voice outranks the general tone rules. If they talk in longer sentences, respect that. If they use a word you would avoid, respect it: it's how they talk.
- Write like someone looking into the camera: short sentences, fragments and everyday contractions ("gonna", "kinda", "y'all"), if that's how they talk.
- Sentences can start with "And", "But", "So" or "Like" when it sounds natural.
- Give rhythm with pauses, emphasis and repetition on purpose. State things: rhetorical questions stay out.
- Use the audience's words and theirs, never words chosen just to sound pretty.
- No bullet lists inside the spoken text.
- Before keeping a line, test it: would they say this looking into the camera? If not, rewrite it.

Three things don't change with the voice:

- The [Floor](floor.md). If their scripts use something the Floor forbids, it doesn't go into the new text. Say in one line that Sofia avoids that pattern and suggest another way.
- Never invent.
- Never copy another person's topic.

## Before delivering

- **VO1.** Every word and expression exists in how the creator talks (profile, approved scripts, samples) or in the everyday speech of their bubble.
- **VO2.** Nothing the creator said they avoid appears in the text.
- **VO3.** Read out loud, the text sounds like their samples.
- **VO4.** A line the creator said in this conversation that went into the text keeps their words.
- **VO5.** The way of addressing the audience (you, you guys, y'all, everyone) is theirs.
- **VO6.** The voice did not knock down any item of the Floor.
