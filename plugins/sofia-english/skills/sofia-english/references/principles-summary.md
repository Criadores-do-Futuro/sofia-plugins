# Virality principles (summary)

The short version of [principles](principles.md). It does not replace the full piece when you write or review an idea.

1. Every video sells an idea: the belief the audience walks away with. Without it clear before writing, the video has no center.
2. The theme sets the ceiling. A universal theme (money, relationships, fear of failure, shame, injustice, belonging) arrives with the emotion ready; perfect technique on a small theme loses.
3. Familiarity lowers the cost of getting in. The audience needs to recognize the situation or the belief, and find something new enough to keep watching.
4. Conflict and change happen in the viewer's head: by showing a turn, by colliding with one of their beliefs, or by waking a conflict they already lived (the strongest).
5. Curiosity is the gap between what they know and what they want to know. Open concrete gaps (what happened, where, how much, with whom) and hold each one for the right time.
6. The aha is worth more after effort. Delivered early, it satisfies without hooking. Withheld on purpose, it takes the debate to comments and shares.
7. Retention guarantees distribution. Mental debate is what crosses bubbles: the viewer compares with their own life, takes a side and shares.
8. To diagnose, in this order: what the idea is, how big the theme is, where the conflict is, where the gaps open and close, when the aha arrives.
9. Before listing what is missing, say what is carrying the video and what could be amplified.
