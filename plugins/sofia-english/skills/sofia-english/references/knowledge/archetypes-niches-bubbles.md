# Content archetypes, niches and bubbles

Use: read BEFORE scripting, to choose the ANGLE (archetype). Each archetype points to the script structure and the principles it tends to trigger.

Corpus: a 12-month discovery of 9 influencers (TikTok and Instagram, 100k+), the Green screen batch (14), the Enrichment batch (13, 2026-08-10) and @alberta.nyc (30 videos from 2026, 100k+).

Patterns taken from real videos with 100k+ views. They help Sofia **choose the angle** and start out knowing which structure and which principles that angle tends to trigger.

## How this file fits into Sofia (4 axes: don't mix them up)

| Axis | Question | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| **Virality principles** | WHY it works | [principles](../principles.md): Theme → Familiarity → Conflict and Change → Curiosity → Aha/Absence. It's the law; everything goes through it. |

## PART 1: CONTENT ARCHETYPES (a repeatable angle)

Each archetype has: **when to use it**, **suggested Sofia structure**, **beats**, **principles it triggers**, **trigger**, **evidence** and **common mistake**.

### A1. Numbered series ("Part N" / "Day N")
- **When to use:** many tricks or items on the same theme; consistency plus retention across videos.
- **Sofia structure:** free (a direct demo in list form); each item can be a mini "Right way".
- **Beats:** (1) collection hook ("Things you didn't know you could do with X, part N") → (2) 1 complete use in seconds → (3) micro-aha → (4) "follow for part N+1".
- **Principles:** Curiosity (permanent open loop) + Familiarity + quick Aha.
- **Trigger:** follow + save.
- **Evidence:** 🟢 @practicalyai 21 videos 100k+ (top 1.7M) · 🟡 @jeldebeloni 1.5–3.8M.
- **Common mistake:** 2 uses in the same video (kills the rhythm); a hook without the number (loses the loop).

### A2. Newsjacking / launch ("just dropped")
- **When to use:** real news (a model, a feature, a product).
- **Sofia structure:** **Analysis** (Conflict/news → Result → Reasons → Moral).
- **Beats:** (1) shock headline → (2) what it is in 1 sentence → (3) counterpoint ("everyone freaked out, but nobody talked about this") → (4) a stated position.
- **Principles:** Theme (current events lend reach) + Conflict by friction + Curiosity.
- **Trigger:** debate/share.
- **Evidence:** 🟢 @alex_intelligence 25.8M and 46.2M; "books" 2.2M.
- **Common mistake:** news with no angle of your own (it becomes a bulletin); "just dropped" when it isn't new.

### A3. Listicle of tools/skills
- **When to use:** curation; content for saves and leads.
- **Sofia structure:** **AIDA** (Attention with a number → Interest in the items → Desire → Action: comment a word).
- **Beats:** (1) hook with a number + status → (2) items **name → what it does → pain it solves** (~7s each; save the best for last) → (3) comment-a-word CTA.
- **Principles:** Curiosity ("the ones that matter") + Identity familiarity (status) + Deliberate absence (the guide in the comments).
- **Trigger:** comment a word → leads.
- **Evidence:** 🟢 @maverickgpt, @ray_fu. Social proof per item raises conversion.
- **Common mistake:** listing with no filter; the gold is the "I tested it and dropped it".

### A4. Income proof / real case
- **When to use:** anchor authority in results; a business or income audience.
- **Sofia structure:** **Identification, Story, Content** (Identification → Story of the system → Content) or AIDA.
- **Beats:** (1) shock proof + confession → (2) anti-pitch ("I'm not going to sell you anything") → (3) mechanism (skill + context) → (4) generous CTA.
- **Principles:** Money theme (high ceiling) + Identity familiarity + Belief conflict.
- **Trigger:** comments + debate ("is it real?").
- **Evidence:** 🟢 @jaredrhod 1.2M / 770k / 465k.
- **Common mistake:** promising income without proof; without the anti-pitch the niche puts its guard up.

### A5. Build in public / community CTA
- **When to use:** grow a community by co-creating.
- **Sofia structure:** **AIDA** (Attention: invitation → Action: comment).
- **Beats:** (1) invitation ("drop your project") → (2) scarcity ("the most-liked comment gets a demo") → (3) follow.
- **Principles:** Direct external engagement (the comment as currency) + Identity familiarity (being part of it).
- **Trigger:** competitive comments + follow.
- **Evidence:** 🟢 @onegirlstack 2.2M.
- **Common mistake:** an invitation with no scarcity or reward.

### A6. Tutorial demo (proof before explanation)
- **When to use:** teach 1 desirable result in an actionable way.
- **Sofia structure:** **Wrong way, right way** (the pain of the manual way → the right way with AI) or an educational AIDA.
- **Beats:** (1) desirable result in the 1st sentence → (2) "look" (show it) → (3) short step by step → (4) expectation management → save.
- **Principles:** Aha delivered (usefulness) + Memory familiarity (the pain of doing that task).
- **Trigger:** save + share.
- **Evidence:** 🟢 @willfrancis24, @coffeewithsimon, @carter.braydon.
- **Common mistake:** explaining before showing; knocking down objections too late.

### A7. Raw proof / live discovery
- **When to use:** maximum identification and authenticity (a friend's tone).
- **Sofia structure:** **Analysis** (Conflict: skepticism → Result → Moral) or Identification, Story, Content.
- **Beats:** (1) starts from distrust + a mundane pain → (2) asks the AI → (3) genuine reaction → (4) a number hidden in the close. No CTA.
- **Principles:** Memory familiarity (the mess) + Conflict in the head (skepticism → proof).
- **Trigger:** identification/debate + "how" questions.
- **Evidence:** 🟢 @will_esl (Brazilian): the most mirrorable template of the Portuguese corpus.
- **Common mistake:** too much production (loses the rawness); close with a real number.

### A8. Controversy / outrage hook
- **When to use:** maximum reach through debate. Use the *mechanics*, honestly.
- **Sofia structure:** **Analysis** (Conflict → Result → 3 Reasons → Moral).
- **Beats:** (1) accusation/shock with a physical verb → (2) bridge "crazier than it looks" → (3) escalation ("the worst part is…") → (4) ends on a statement, with no CTA.
- **Principles:** Universal conflict (injustice) + Curiosity + Absence of aha (the debate moves outside the video).
- **Trigger:** outraged comments and taking sides.
- **Evidence:** 🔴 @candresperedo 15.1M; 🟢 @alex_intelligence "books".
- **Common mistake (ethics warning):** inventing a fact to shock. Rule number one: **never invent**. Copy the structure, not the lie.

### A9. Authority / aspirational advice
- **When to use:** positioning; a business or personal development audience.
- **Sofia structure:** **Identification, Story, Content** or **Them, Me, You, Future**.
- **Beats:** (1) provocative question or authority quote → (2) insight/clip → (3) aspirational identity.
- **Principles:** Identity familiarity + Curiosity + Money/status theme.
- **Trigger:** save + aspirational share.
- **Evidence:** 🔴 @steven (Bartlett) 100k–10M.
- **Common mistake:** a shallow insight; the question on screen needs to open a real gap.

### A10. Comparison / showdown (X vs Y)
- **When to use:** a theme with "fans" (tools, models, brands).
- **Sofia structure:** **Wrong way, right way** or **Analysis**.
- **Beats:** (1) confrontation ("I dropped ChatGPT; Claude does X") → (2) the same task side by side → (3) concrete verdict → leave the debate open.
- **Principles:** Conflict (fandom) + Familiarity (both brands).
- **Trigger:** fandom debate + save.
- **Evidence:** 🟢 @alex_intelligence (Kimi vs Claude/ChatGPT).
- **Common mistake:** a comparison with no clear verdict; a metric the audience doesn't care about.

### A11. Sketch with personified AI brands
- **When to use:** maximum sharing through humor plus an uncomfortable truth; Brazilian audience in the corpus.
- **Sofia structure:** sketch/dialogue (outside the 5; apply the principles directly).
- **Beats:** (1) personified AIs (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) in a dialogue → (2) they expose a social truth → (3) a punch that escalates the absurdity.
- **Principles:** Familiarity (the brands) + Conflict (satire) + humor/identification.
- **Trigger:** **share** + debate.
- **Evidence:** 🟡 @lara_santana 513k / 7.9k reposts.
- **Common mistake:** a joke with no truth behind it; a character the audience doesn't recognize.

### A12. Aspirational advice / mindset (share engine)
- **When to use:** break out of the bubble through a universal bridge; content the viewer **sends to someone**.
- **Sofia structure:** a short **Analysis** (thesis → reason → moral) or a direct monologue.
- **Beats:** (1) promise of transformation + "listen to what I'm telling you" → (2) **one single thesis** → (3) **a concrete, physical instruction** → (4) the antithesis of a belief. Usually no CTA.
- **Principles:** Familiarity + Belief conflict + Aspirational identity familiarity.
- **Trigger:** share/repost + save.
- **Evidence:** 🔴 @eusouroberth 1.0M / **18.4k reposts**.
- **Common mistake:** abstract advice without the physical instruction; more than one thesis per video.

### A13. Debate / public bet
- **When to use:** hold through the tension of a confrontation; themes with two sides.
- **Sofia structure:** **Wrong way, right way** (the argument) or Analysis.
- **Beats:** (1) controversial statement → (2) live comeback → (3) **public bet** → (4) close with **back-of-the-napkin math**.
- **Principles:** Conflict (the argument) + Curiosity + Money theme + proof by number.
- **Trigger:** debate + share.
- **Evidence:** 🔴 @musametas 3.8M.
- **Common mistake:** an empty bet; napkin math without an honest number.

### A14. Scene sketch: 1 person, 2+ characters (satire of your own bubble)
- **When to use:** maximum sharing; satirizing your niche's culture.
- **Sofia structure:** sketch/dialogue (outside the 5). If you want an argument skeleton, fit the scene into a **Wrong way, right way** (the "wrong" is the scene; the "right" is the moral).
- **Beats:** (1) open **in the middle** of an absurd scene, with a shock line → (2) the other character answers with even shakier logic → (3) escalation → (4) hard cut, no CTA. Underneath, a real truth.
- **Principles:** Memory familiarity (a recognizable scene) + Conflict (contrast) + humor.
- **Trigger:** identification + **share**.
- **Evidence:** 🟢/🔴 @alberta.nyc: appy-bill 1.3M, illegal-hiring 1.3M, stole-jobs-egos 715k (2026).
- **Common mistake:** a joke with no truth; sticking in a CTA (kills the share).

### A15. Quick contrarian hot take (straight to camera)
- **When to use:** debate + share; one controversial thesis in a burst.
- **Sofia structure:** an accelerated **Analysis** (thesis → reasons → moral).
- **Beats:** (1) counterintuitive thesis in the 1st sentence → (2) 2–4 quick arguments, each sharper than the last → (3) a dry close.
- **Principles:** Belief conflict + Curiosity (how will they defend it?) + rhythm.
- **Trigger:** debate + share.
- **Evidence:** 🟢/🔴 @alberta.nyc: hand-written 265k, grok-twitter 529k, bobby-tables 156k.
- **Common mistake:** a lukewarm thesis; slow justification.

> **Cross-cutting principle (@alberta.nyc):** every joke or satire **wraps a real truth** of the audience. Humor without truth doesn't get shared. The news used is **real**: the exaggeration is the joke, never the fact.

### Hook sub-patterns (variations that already went viral)
- **Secret document/report + authority:** "So-and-so published a document nobody read" + **plant a sentence and promise to come back to it**. 🟢 @maestrosdaia.
- **"Forget [paid], use [free]":** drop the expensive one → name of the free one → demo → climax "100% free". 🟢 @jackroberts.
- **Myth vs truth (careful with the shortcut):** give voice to the belief → "be very careful" → why. 🟢 @rodrigotadewald.
- **New skill + non-obvious use:** news → "the best use isn't the obvious one" → the insight becomes the hook. 🟢 @jackroberts 135k.
- **Alarm news (journalistic):** a neutral framing that escalates with real numbers. 🔴 @bbcnews 1.4M. Honest.

### Retention playbook (storytelling): @mino.mp4 (50M)
Apply inside any narrative archetype:
1. **Hook** = a specific goal + conflict.
2. **Click confirmation** = show the protagonist acting (most people skip this).
3. **Second conflict** = a fall in the middle.
4. **Climax** = the turn.
5. **Lesson** for the audience.
> Golden rule: **plant a second conflict in the middle**: it holds retention after the hook.

## PART 3: By BUBBLE (sets the reach ceiling)

> Bubble = a group of people super interested in one specific subject (see [breaking into a bubble](../breaking-the-bubble.md): what it is, the 6 types and the name test). The bigger the bubble, the higher the ceiling and the lower the specific commercial intent. The table below compares the ceiling of a few bubbles.

| Bubble | Ceiling | Commercial intent | How you get in | Universal bridge |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **AI / Claude** | Medium (growing) | High | Directly (your niche) | Money, time, fear |
| **Tech / smartphone** | Very high | Medium | "you don't use even 1% of AI" | Utilitarian curiosity |
| **Money / business** | Maximum | High | "income with AI" | Universal |
| **Productivity / work** | High | Medium-high | "cut the grunt work" / fear for your job | Tiredness/time/fear |
| **Celebrity / drama** | Maximum (short-lived) | Low | Only occasional newsjacking | Outrage/curiosity |

**Decision rule:** choose the bubble through step 1 of [breaking into a bubble](../breaking-the-bubble.md): first familiarity (a bubble the creator already lives in day to day), then closeness to the goal. The ceiling comes in as a comparison between the bubbles that pass those two criteria. To connect the bubble to what the creator sells, find the common ground between the two circles (the Rubik's cube); if the theme is a niche one (AI), a universal bridge (money, time, fear) is often that common ground.

## How Sofia uses this file (when scripting)

1. **Bubble and ceiling** of the theme (Part 3) + the universal bridge.
2. **Niche and archetype** (Parts 2 and 1) that serve the idea the video sells.
3. **Script structure** (the one suggested by the archetype, among the 5 in [structures](../structures/README.md)).
4. **Principles** ([principles](../principles.md)): check Theme, Familiarity, Conflict/Change, Curiosity and where the Aha closes or stays open.
5. **Recording format** with the creator: choose in [formats](../formats/README.md) (the README pairs archetype with format) and follow the "Writing rules" of the chosen file.
6. **Trigger** consistent with the goal (reach = debate/share; lead = comment a word; retention = follow) and the archetype's **common mistake**.
