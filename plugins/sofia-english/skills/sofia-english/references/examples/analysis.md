# Analyzed examples: Analysis format

The quoted script lines below are translated from the Portuguese originals; links, numbers and names are unchanged. Brackets in brackets ([ ]) explain the line's job and why it works. Use these to learn the shape, never to copy the lines.

Two real scripts by @hanahfranklin, the most-liked of 13 videos in this format. Literal transcription (speech slips corrected in brackets in the original). Structure: [Analysis](../structures/analysis.md). Format: [analysis](../formats/analysis.md).

## Example 1: Professor Crônicas (133K likes)

**Link:** https://www.instagram.com/reel/DPAM7t8jWR5/
**Length:** 129 s · ~470 words · subject's voice ≈ 12%

---

[HOOK CLIP from the subject, 3 s: mass-appeal theme (taxes), understandable with no context, punchline at the end]
*(clip)* "Only people with no contacts in their phone pay income tax."

[LABEL, comic fake news: an absurd accusation about something harmless, in a list of 3 where the 3rd item is a "popular truth" the audience would sign]
This teacher is under investigation for teaching money laundering, managing your side pieces, and that whether it's left or right, it's always the people who get f...

[The subject's catchphrase ("Let's break it down?") used as a bridge: familiarity for anyone who's seen him]
Let's break it down?

[Teasing the viewer as a joke: creates belonging for those "in the know"]
If you don't know Professor Crônicas, it's because you're broke and don't pay for Instapremium.

[Label turns into praise: the "on the internet" superlative]
Because this guy delivers the most absurd lessons on the internet.

[CREDENTIAL: rounded number + a unique detail ("just this year") that turns a number into a story]
Gained over 600 thousand followers just this year.

[List of 3 with a comic anticlimax in the 3rd item]
Teaches everything you need to know about politics, philosophy and smut.

[PROMISE at 26 s, with the subject as the grammatical subject; "three" sets up a countdown; catchphrase "dominate the Brazilian internet"]
That's why today we're going to analyze the three principles Professor Crônicas uses to dominate the Brazilian internet.

[PRINCIPLE 1, named from the subject himself, with censored profanity: nobody forgets it]
The Son of a B**** is a nerd.

[Proof in a list of 3 with anticlimax: serious, serious, absurd]
He quotes the French Revolution. Knows Einstein, Plato and Aristotle personally. And above all, has the most educational class on the internet about money laundering.

[Proof clip: the principle happens on its own, no explanation]
*(clip)* "Only people who don't know how to launder money wash their hair. Who said that? Jeff Bezos."

[Fan reaction: the presenter doesn't tell the joke, reacts to it]
I keep wondering how he holds so much knowledge.

[MECHANISM by inversion + common villain: "it might look like just X, but it has more Y than most Z who..."]
Because it might look like just goofing around, but this guy's lessons have more true information than most content creators who post screenshots of scientific studies they don't even read.

[LESSON as a scolding to the viewer (intimate "you"): the analysis becomes their diagnosis]
Explains why nobody watches your videos. Since you're lazy, don't do research and never deliver content that makes people feel they learned something new.

[PRINCIPLE 2: highbrow + pop + lowbrow trio ("Choquei" is a Brazilian gossip page); "Because yes" skips justification, best-friend tone]
Two. Us? Culture, Choquei and college parties. Because yes.

[Proof by contrast: "the same guy who [3 serious things] is the same guy who [3 lowbrow things]"]
The same guy who knows law, history and philosophy is the same guy who literally craps his pants, jumps in the mud and knows everything about the boy Neymar's cheating.

[Proof clip with the punchline at the end (Machado de Assis is a classic Brazilian novelist; Capitu and Bentinho are characters from his novel)]
*(clip)* "If Neymar DMs my girlfriend, would she hook up with him? Who asked that was Machado de Assis. That explains it. Capitu cheated on Bentinho with Neymar."

[MECHANISM named and tied to the result ("growing so much")]
It also explains why Crônicas is growing so much. Because he connects the depth few people care about with the nonsense the internet loves.

[Imperative LESSON for "you"]
So stop being boring, talking only about your story, niche or product. And connect it to something emotionally relevant to people.

[PRINCIPLE 3 = the product's pillar (creative format), named with the nickname of the subject's format ("Telecurso 2000" is an old Brazilian educational TV show)]
Three. Creative format Telecurso 2000.

[MECHANISM with the presenter's own branded concept ("what I call the addiction loop") + catchphrase "repeat, repeat, repeat"]
It's practically impossible to watch one of his videos, like it and ignore the next one. Because he creates what I call the addiction loop. He has a format, a content pattern that works, and he repeats, repeats, repeats. That way, when you watch one video, you want to watch them all. Because you know it'll be good.

[Generalization: the subject becomes proof of a general law]
That's how he and the biggest content creators in the country have such a high view average.

[TURN, mirror question: the viewer goes from audience to patient (in an English script, state it instead; see the Floor)]
Could it be that a creative format is what's missing from your content?

[CTA with a callback to the subject: "too bad he doesn't teach... but I do"]
It's really a shame the professor doesn't teach you to find your format and write the script. But I do. Want to learn with me? Just tap the link in my bio.

## Example 2: Edi Ribeiro (114K likes, 950 comments)

**Link:** https://www.instagram.com/reel/DTyj4u8DX2A/
**Length:** 154 s · ~520 words

---

[HOOK CLIP: the subject's own self-irony about her body (the sensitive joke is HERS, not the presenter's)]
*(clip)* "This isn't a skirt, it's a footstool."

[LABEL: "on the internet" superlative + a second trait with a villain (the hater)]
This woman has the most unbeatable self-love on the internet. And a way of talking that leaves haters embarrassed and speechless.

[Short proof clip for the label]
*(clip)* "See this right here? I don't fit through, I don't fit through. This right here is every chubby girl's nightmare."

[CREDENTIAL: big → bigger ("and counting" adds movement) + fan reaction]
She has 3 million followers and counting. Videos with over 20 million views. And every time she posts, I'm like this, look.

[PROMISE at 23 s (variant: "breakdown of the viral format")]
That's why this is the breakdown of the viral format for you to get inspired and dominate the Brazilian internet.

[PRINCIPLE 1: named from the subject's format (jokes, lesson, inspiration) + general law in a trio]
Jokes, lesson and inspiration. To make it on the internet today, you'll inevitably need one of these 3 things: teach, entertain or inspire. But Edi is a genius at doing all 3.

[Proof: each item in the trio illustrated by a clip that happens on its own]
She makes you laugh by mocking her own problems. *(clip)* "Girl, I don't even fit on the screen, huh?" She literally teaches self-esteem and emotional intelligence. *(clip)* "Me, with this mud belly of mine, not going to the beach, not putting on a bikini... Either you want to lose weight or you want to live. I do both."

[Chained BRIDGE, not just numbered]
Which already connects to pillar number 2:

[PRINCIPLE 2: reused branded concept ("journey effect") + anaphora "we see / freak out / cheer" (belonging)]
Journey effect. Every week we see her improving at sports. We freak out with every new outfit she shows. And above all, we cheer every step she takes in her weight-loss process.

[MECHANISM as a ladder: "telling is nice, but living it together is a thousand times more powerful"]
Edi already impacts a lot of lives with her videos today. But I can't stop imagining how many lives she'll change when she finishes the process. Because telling a comeback story is nice, but it's a thousand times more powerful when you live a transformation journey together with your people.

[PRINCIPLE 3 = the product's pillar, named after the subject's format; broken into 3 predictions]
Three: creative format "get ready with me." When Edi shows up with a new Shein outfit, we already know 3 things. First, she'll start the video looking a mess, but only to create contrast. Second, she'll yell at me like her problems are my fault. And finally, her videos are good, because if you watch one, you'll have to watch them all, because you already know it'll be good.

[Generalization with catchphrase]
Edi and the biggest content creators in the country grow nonstop after finding a format and repeating, repeating, repeating.

[TURN: scolding with the year ("in 2026")]
It makes no sense! You're creating content in 2026 without trying to find your creative format?

[CTA: social proof ladder (10, 30, 50, 100 thousand) + a free offer (the presenter's paid offer is summarized here)]
Over the last few weeks I've helped hundreds of people in the hardest niches hit 10, 30, 50, even 100 thousand followers using this strategy. And if you want to learn how to find your format, just tap the link in my bio, I left something free for you there.

## What both have in common (and the weak videos don't)

- The hook is a clip of the subject, ≤ 4 s. The only one of the 13 that opened with the presenter talking jargon got 7 thousand likes.
- Followers spoken before the promise, and the promise between 19 and 26 s.
- At least one principle named from the subject ("the SOB is a nerd," "Telecurso 2000," "get ready with me").
- The last principle is the pillar the product sells, and the CTA returns to the subject.
- The presenter almost never talks about themselves before the close. The three least-liked videos have too much "I."

## Adapting this for a US creator

Keep the mechanics (clip, label, credential, promise, three named principles, turn, ask) and replace every name, number and reference with a real subject the US audience knows, with sources (see [facts and sources](../facts-and-sources.md)). Never reuse these lines or their numbers.
