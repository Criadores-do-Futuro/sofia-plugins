# Commander's intent

Commander's intent is the central goal and main message of each piece of content: the idea the creator wants to plant in the audience's mind. It's the thread that ties their positioning to their offer. In [breaking into a bubble](breaking-the-bubble.md), it's the second circle, the sales seed. In [theme and moral](addictive-elements/theme-and-moral.md), it's the moral.

## 1. Why it matters

- **Not getting lost.** With a clear intent, every video knows where it's going, even when the theme moves to another bubble.
- **Going viral without vanity.** Reach that plants nothing is just a number. Commander's intent turns reach into positioning and sales: selling while going viral.
- **Subtle selling.** Every piece of content sells, without pushing. The sale comes from the planted idea, and the direct ask shows up rarely.

## 2. The 3 pillars

1. **The leader's Authenticity:** the creator is also the product. The viewer buys the person before the offer.
2. **Movement:** a cause bigger than the leader, with a common enemy. The audience joins the movement and sees themselves in it.
3. **Product:** the creator's unique mechanisms. The viewer needs to see a new solution to an old problem, one that explains why the solutions that came before failed.

## 3. How to build it with the creator

In conversation, one question at a time. Each answer is saved to the creator profile only with their OK (section 5).

1. **Intentional slice.** The profile shows a slice of their real personality, chosen on purpose to strengthen the business's positioning. They don't need to expose 100% of their life, like an open diary. Agree on what from their life and work goes into the videos and what stays out. Whatever they don't want to show stays out.
2. **Proof stories.** For each pillar, the question: "Which real stories from my life, my community or my clients prove this point?" Each pillar needs at least one.
3. **Brain dump.** They pour out everything they know and do about the subject, with no filter. Then group it into 3 or 4 blocks: each block becomes a product mechanism with its own name.
4. **Personal turn.** "What did you live through in the past that you overcame, and today you look at the market and feel it needs to change?" The answer gives the movement's ideal and its common enemy.

## 4. What to define in each pillar

### Authenticity

- Random interests: specific hobbies and tastes (like music, pets, art or dance) that create everyday points of connection with the viewer.
- Books, movies, creators and brands they admire.
- Safe vulnerabilities: real struggles or traits they're willing to show to feel more human (like anxiety or fears they overcame), without overexposing themselves.
- 5 of their traits, each with a visible action in the video (examples: waking up early to show discipline; using "meta-teaching" to show technical brilliance).
- The inner child: what they loved doing as a kid that still shows up in them today.

### Movement

- The ideal: the world the movement wants to build.
- The common enemy: the behavior, belief or way of doing things the movement fights (example: "normosis", the normal that makes people sick).
- The community's name (an invented example: "the Night Owls").
- Symbols and rituals: images that represent the movement (example: a crystal ball to predict the future) and practices that repeat (examples: guided breathing, the community's own greetings).
- Its own language: catchphrases and expressions (examples: "It's time for action!", "Aha!").
- Non-negotiable beliefs (examples: "The future belongs to the bold", "Knowledge kills insecurity", "Whoever has fun doesn't quit").

### Product

- The mechanisms with their own names, from the brain dump blocks.
- The differentiators.
- The competition map: what competitors offer, what promise they make, what the audience criticizes about them and what the creator disagrees with.

## 5. Where to keep it

In the creator profile, one section per pillar, only after the creator's OK:

- "Commander's intent: Authenticity";
- "Commander's intent: Movement";
- "Commander's intent: Product".

Always use these titles. When the host has file tools and the creator agrees, they live in the creator profile file (`sofia-english/styles/<profile>.md`); otherwise keep them in the conversation and say they weren't saved. With the same title, the section is updated and what the creator says later is added at the end. Inside each section, one `##` per pillar item, like the columns of a board, in the creator's own words. Read the three sections before writing an idea, a script, an adjustment or a critique.

## 6. In the script

- **One seed per script.** Each video plants a single idea, from one pillar. The video brief's idea states the pillar and the sentence that stays in the viewer's head, in one line: "Seed (Movement): [the sentence]".
- **Raising awareness.** Seeds add up over the weeks. A viewer who watches a sequence of videos gains a layer of awareness with each one. When they meet the offer, they already recognize the value of each step of the method.
- **Authenticity and movement enter inside the story,** with no pause for an ad. Narrative hooks and deliberate jokes: sarcastic expressions, personal analogies or quick mentions of the routine (example: a lighthearted comment about their tastes, or the cat walking through the shot).
- **The close reinforces a belief of the movement** (example: "Stop being just a spectator and become a content creator").
- **Hidden CTA, in most videos.** The offer appears inside a story, without asking for anything. Examples: "Last week, someone in my community asked me...", "When I was working with a client face to face...", "In my last consultation, I noticed a pattern...". The viewer instantly registers the creator's authority and that there's a service they can hire. The story must have happened. Without a real case, use another kind of CTA.
- **Direct CTA, in about 2 out of every 10 videos,** and in stories. Example: "I have a method built around exactly this. Tap the link in my bio to grab your spot." A fixed sign-off at the end of the video also counts. When unsure how many direct CTAs have already gone out, ask the creator.

## 7. Before delivering

- **CI1.** The script plants a single seed, from one pillar, written in the video brief's idea.
- **CI2.** The seed comes from the profile's notes or from what the creator said in the conversation, with no invented mechanism, story or belief.
- **CI3.** Authenticity and movement show up inside the story, with no pause for an ad.
- **CI4.** The close reinforces a belief of the movement.
- **CI5.** The call to action has a type: hidden (with a real case) or direct (about 2 out of every 10 videos).
