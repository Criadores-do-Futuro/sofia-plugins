Sophia English

Scripts in your own voice

A Sophia for analyzing references, writing scripts and improving your existing work.

What you can ask for

  • Analyze a video or transcript with evidence.
  • Write a script using your facts and voice.
  • Review without inventing flaws or results.
  • Learn your style and export your materials.

Works with Codex and compatible Claude environments. Local visual analysis requires Python and FFmpeg; text work does not need these programs.

Purchases of subscriptions, community memberships or courses take place through Hotmart. This plugin does not process payments.
